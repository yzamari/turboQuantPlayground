## Speedup table (q4_0 KV / f16 KV, both with flash attention)

| depth | f16 tg64 t/s | q4_0 tg64 t/s | speedup |
|------:|-------------:|--------------:|--------:|
|     0 |        26.18 |         24.75 |   0.95x |
|  1024 |        17.56 |         19.89 |  *1.13x* |
|  4096 |        10.13 |         11.76 |  *1.16x* |

## Interpretation

LLM decode is memory-bandwidth-bound on phones. At depth 0 there is no KV cache
yet, so compressing it cannot help; the very small 0.95x at d=0 is dequant
overhead in the read path. As depth grows, the FP16 KV cache becomes the
dominant memory traffic per generated token, and shrinking it 4x recovers a
proportional fraction of throughput: 1.13x at 1024 tokens, 1.16x at 4096
tokens, growing from there.

This is the q4_0-KV cousin of what TurboQuant delivers. q4_0 KV gets the same
4x compression ratio but with cosine ~0.85 vs FP16 attention. TurboQuant
achieves 4x at cosine 0.92 (measured on this device, real Llama-3.2-1B
layers — see tabs9p-llama32-1b-realmodel.txt). Once we land Path 2 (a
llama_kv_cache_turboquant subclass that streams decode through our compressed
representation), tok/s should match or exceed the q4_0 numbers above with
markedly higher quality.

       -d 0,1024,4096 -ctk f16 -ctv f16 -fa 1 -r 3 --no-warmup'
